# M1b — Auto-shaping

Implemented 3 November 2026. Open `Unity/Assets/_Project/Ship/Scenes/ShipYard.unity` and press Play. The initial ship is still one hull block. No M1c work was started.

**Built:** corner-based hull tiles, with all 256 occupancy patterns mapped to 22 canonical shapes through 48 rotations/reflections; faceted convex chamfers; live Inspector Chamfer Size (default 0.5 m); H comparison against plain cubes; undoable/saved hull locks and paint; eight muted colours; fixed Window, Door frame and Ramp parts. Locked hulls use cubes with a small top corner mark in Build mode. Hull collision remains box geometry; the frame has three solid bars around a 2 m wide × 2.25 m high opening, and the ramp has a convex 45° collider (below the controller's existing 58° slope limit). Drop-in also handles the ramp slope.

**Keys:** B build/drop in; 1 Hull, 2 Window, 3 Door frame, 4 Ramp; R/T/G rotate fixed pieces and cube previews; H shaping on/off; L toggles the aimed hull's lock; [ / ] chooses paint; middle click paints. Left/right click places/removes, Z/Y undoes/redoes, F5/F9 saves/loads. Existing movement keys remain unchanged.

**Decisions:** tiles cut only into occupied cells: empty-cell intrusion is 0 m. Concave corners stay sharp. Each octant takes its owning cell's colour, giving clean, hard colour boundaries without averaging. Fixed pieces count as solid for the pattern; supplemental hull faces close their hollow interfaces, including the floor under a door opening. Part rotation is ignored on shaped hulls. A local edit rebuilds only affected corner/face-neighbour visual chunks and its own collision chunk; H and the global chamfer slider intentionally rebuild the whole visual layer. Door frame mass is 80 kg; Ramp is 120 kg. The door's front/back airtight flags are off; no sealing logic was added.

**Light checks:** Unity compiled; all 22 tiles rendered. Exercised H, lock, paint, rotated fixed-piece placement and undo/redo. A 503-block mixed-part save round-tripped byte-for-byte across fresh Play sessions. Local placement/removal/history checks around 500 blocks showed no obvious pause; no profiling or review runners were used. Walked from the pad up the ramp, through the frame and inside a one-cell-high room; H did not affect walking. Ramp drop-in passed. M0 received a movement/jump Play mode smoke check. Original ship save restored byte-for-byte. One native editor startup crash cleared on reopening; the final checks had no new script/shader/runtime errors.
