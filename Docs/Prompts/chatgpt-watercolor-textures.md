# Watercolour test textures for the Shakedown cockpit

I'm making **Shakedown**, a calm, cosy first-person space exploration game. The art style is low-poly 3D with **hand-painted watercolour textures**. I'm testing whether this works on a cockpit model in Blender, and I need six preliminary textures.

Please generate them **one at a time**, in the order below. After each one, briefly tell me what you made, then continue with the next.

## Rules for textures 1–5 (tiling textures)

- **Seamless and tileable:** the left edge continues into the right edge, the top into the bottom. This is the most important rule; please check the edges before you finish each one.
- **Flat, front-on view:** no perspective, no painted lighting or shadows, no highlights, no text, no frame or border.
- **Greyscale and fairly light:** mostly 70–90% brightness, with only small darker accents. The colour comes from the 3D material later, so one texture must work under any tint.
- **Watercolour look:** soft washes, visible paper grain and brush texture, slightly uneven pigment, gentle bleeding edges.
- **Quiet and even:** no single dominant blotch or feature that would stand out when the texture repeats many times.
- **Square, 1024 × 1024.**

## The textures

1. **Hull plating** (exterior of the ship, repeats every 2 m): large soft rectangular panels, about 1 m each, so 2 × 2 panels per tile. Faint seams between panels, a few small rivets along the seams, gentle wear near the edges.
2. **Interior wall** (walls and ceiling inside, repeats every 2 m): calmer than the hull. Very subtle panel lines, mostly an even, soft wash. It should feel cosy and quiet.
3. **Floor** (repeats every 2 m): slightly more structure: a hint of square floor tiles or fine grating, light scuffs and footpath wear. Can be a little darker than the walls (60–80% brightness).
4. **Console metal** (consoles and side panels, repeats every 1 m): a smooth, slightly mottled surface with fine, sparse scratches. Calm and even.
5. **Upholstery** (the command chair, repeats every 0.5 m): a soft fabric or worn leather feel, with a faint hint of weave or stitching. Very even.

## Texture 6 (not tiling)

6. **Star map screen** (shown on the main console, glowing): a watercolour star map on a dark background. Soft painted nebula clouds in teal and violet, small star dots, a few thin route lines connecting stars, and two or three small circled destinations. Wider than tall (16:9). This one **may use colour** and does **not** need to tile. No text or labels.
