# Indie explorer interior direction

Two concept images generated with the built-in image generation tool. The user's direction is a grounded first-person space RPG interior interpreted through modest indie production scope and watercolor materials. Images are visual concepts, not in-engine screenshots or validated performance targets.

## Cockpit

Use case: stylized-concept. One landscape 16:9 image imagining an ACTUAL FIRST-PERSON INDIE SPACE RPG GAME SCREENSHOT, with the grounded, walkable, functional NASA-inspired spacecraft feeling associated with Starfield, reinterpreted by a small studio with modest resources and a love of watercolor textures. Original spacecraft design. Clearly rendered 3D space with solid depth, occlusion, broad simple polygonal meshes, flat/modestly beveled shapes and repeating modular construction. Hand-painted watercolor diffuse textures ON the 3D geometry: soft pigment washes, edge pooling, paper grain, simplified painted wear. Crucially the scene must read as an achievable in-engine Unity indie game environment, NOT a flat watercolor illustration or a watercolor filter over a photoreal image. No outlines around everything. Selective functional detail and a few reusable prop types, readable silhouettes and uncluttered composition, restrained warm/cool lighting achievable with a few lights. More practical and utilitarian than the supplied reference interiors, which are only palette and watercolor references. Cream/off-white modular panels, charcoal floor and structural ribs, faded sage/teal equipment, small ochre accents. Visible modular ribs, a few bundled cable conduits, inset vents, accessible maintenance panels. Quiet lived-in exploration mood. No people, text, labels, logos, watermark, HUD, border, weapons, glossy photoreal materials, dense tiny greebles or luxury yacht styling.
COCKPIT: Camera is the standing player's eye-level viewpoint just inside the rear hatch, slightly to one side, looking toward a single pilot's seat and forward windows; credible room-sized playable space. Compact cockpit roughly 3.5m wide, recessed single captain's chair with simple angular dark fabric cushions and armrests, horseshoe console around its front. Three chunky reusable instrument screens recessed into sloping console, one dark teal star navigation map, two basic diagram/status displays without lettering. A joystick, a small handful of tactile toggle/button clusters, not hundreds of individually modeled switches. Broad angular forward windows through thick charcoal mullions reveal a softly painted planetary horizon in orbit. Exposed structural frame and overhead utility conduit make it a working ship, a simple handrail by the chair, one clipped logbook and mug in a holder give personality. Show believable walkway and ability to enter pilot chair. Practical muted off-white panel construction, sage metal surfaces and warm amber task lights, watercolor texture does the aesthetic work. View should feel like the player could take one step forward and sit down. Sharp readable modeled forms with visible painterly materials, not a concept art wash.
