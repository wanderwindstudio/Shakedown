## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Generate ONE exterior concept image of a COOL MODULAR EXPLORER SPACESHIP for Shakedown, a cosy indie space exploration game. The key design challenge: the ship is assembled from SEVERAL ROOM-SIZED COMPARTMENTS OF ROUGHLY THE SAME SIZE, yet has a deliberate dynamic memorable spacecraft silhouette. Show the modular construction unmistakably. Use six equal-sized chamfered rectangular pressure modules, each roughly 3m wide x 3m long x 2.8m high, sharing the same structural frame dimensions and connector collars. Three modules form a central longitudinal spine: forward cockpit compartment, central connecting habitat compartment, aft engineering compartment. Two additional identical-volume modules are attached directly to the sides of the middle/aft spine, staggered fore/aft by one connector bay to create a purposeful asymmetric exploration ship silhouette. A sixth equal module sits one level up over the aft spine, giving a low stepped dorsal profile. Modules attach flush at their bulkheads with visible narrow recessed joint lines and matching structural frames; all form one connected walkable vessel, not disconnected floating boxes. The cockpit has sloping glazing and small nose trim but occupies the same base envelope as other room modules. Distinguish functions through a round cabin porthole, cargo hatch, workshop vents and cockpit windows, not different room sizes. Equal repeating cross sections must be readable in perspective. Unify the arrangement with a few narrow sage structural rails, small chamfered corner fairings and a restrained repeated trim stripe, without hiding the compartment boundaries. Twin compact aft propulsion units, short stabilizer fins and small maneuvering thrusters give it a nimble capable look. Small dish/sensor cluster. Four sturdy deployed landing legs, one modest entrance stair. NO gigantic single monolithic central hull, no long container train, no random tower of blocks, no huge wings, no weapons. A believable little home and expedition tool with attitude, attractive and original. Art direction: simple low-poly flat shaded 3D forms, matte warm cream-white hull, faded teal/sage frame and trim, charcoal joints, warm amber lights; hand-painted WATERCOLOUR textures with paper grain, pigment pooling and soft washes on modeled surfaces. Reference image is ONLY a palette, watercolor material and cozy scale reference; create a NEW visibly modular silhouette rather than repeat its monolithic hull. One intact exterior view, no cutaway, no internal rooms, no exploded diagram. Show the entire craft from a slightly elevated front-left three-quarter view so several compartment roofs, stepped arrangement and joint boundaries are clearly visible. Ship fills most of landscape 16:9 frame, all extremities inside frame. Landed on a simple rocky alien plateau with soft lavender distant terrain and pale dusk sky, unobtrusive background. No people, text, labels, numbers, watermark or border. Prioritize equal module scale, readable joins, coherent cool silhouette, and watercolor indie aesthetic.
