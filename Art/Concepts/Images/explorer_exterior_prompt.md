## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Generate ONE exterior concept image of the small spacecraft whose FOUR INTERIOR reference images are supplied. References are design and material references, not images to edit. This is Shakedown: a calm cosy low-poly space exploration game. Imagine the complete exterior around those interiors: a small nimble one-person explorer with enough actual enclosed hull volume for the single-seat bridge, compact sleeping/dining cabin, tiny cargo bay and galley workshop with fabricator, separator and hydroponics. About 12–14 metres long, roughly 5 metres wide main hull, one deck, compact rather than a capital ship. Cohesive charming recognisable silhouette: tapered faceted nose with the broad three-part angular cockpit windscreen from reference 1, a slightly fuller chamfered central habitation hull behind, small round side porthole matching reference 3, short swept stabilizer fins, two compact rear engine nacelles tucked close to hull, discreet maneuvering thrusters, a small roof sensor and antenna. Matte cream-white hull with desaturated sage/teal trim, soft grey undersides, restrained panel seams and little edge wear. A small inset side boarding hatch with short deployed steps, subtle aft cargo access, believable landing struts and feet. Constructed of simple clearly visible low-poly flat-shaded forms with HAND-PAINTED WATERCOLOUR TEXTURES: delicate paper grain, softly uneven pigment, gentle watercolor bleeding and washes, exactly the gentle material feel of the interior references. Warm amber cabin light behind windows, dark muted teal/violet glazing. Show the WHOLE SHIP in a clear front-left three-quarter view, camera slightly elevated so its nose, side, roof and rear nacelle are readable; do not crop any part. Landed on a quiet low-poly rocky plateau at lavender dusk with sparse grass, distant softened hills and a faint ringed planet in sky. Spacecraft occupies most of the frame, environment subdued and minimal. Soft natural light, welcoming exploratory mood. Landscape 16:9. Exterior only, intact opaque hull, no cutaway, no exposed interior rooms. No people, weapons, text, labels, registration numbers, logos, watermark, border. Avoid photorealism, glossy metallic finish, excessive greebles, huge wings, fighter-jet proportions, oversized engines and toy-miniature scale.
